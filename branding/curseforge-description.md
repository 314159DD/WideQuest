## Description

A wide quest log for WoW Forever with a map right next to it.

## Features

- **Wide quest log:** your quests on the left, quest text, objectives and rewards on the right.
- **Quest map:** select a quest and the map next to the log shows its zone and where to go.
- **Quests to Pick Up:** quests you can take right now for the zone on the map.
- **Things to Bring:** items the zone's quests ask for, with how many you carry.

## Questie

Works on its own. With Questie installed, the map also shows Questie's quest icons and the two lists under the map fill in.

## Options

Type **/widequest**, or open Options, AddOns, Wide Quest Log Forever. Drag the window by its title bar and resize it with the grip at the bottom.
